# Medical data privacy

## Scientific motivation

Identifying information may be embedded in DICOM private tags, image burn-ins or linked records.

## Concrete evaluation design

Treat toy metadata filters as insufficient for medical de-identification. Compare with appropriate baselines. Describe the data-generating mechanism, clinical label semantics, statistical unit of analysis and evaluation conditions rather than treating every scan as independent.

## Research interpretation

Log model versions, random seeds and provenance. Analyze error cases, assumptions and possible distribution shift; perform external replication if evidence supports further investigation. **This is a research handbook entry—not completed experimental evidence or medical advice.**
