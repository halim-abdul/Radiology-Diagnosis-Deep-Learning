# Domain adaptation

## Scientific motivation

Scanner and institution shift may alter low-level appearance without changing underlying anatomy.

## Mathematical or methodological point

Feature alignment is not sufficient when label prevalence or definitions shift.

## Experimental design

Maintain a fully untouched external validation site. When designing a benchmark, document the source population, dataset provenance, validation design, key assumptions, and whether this hypothesis has actually been tested.

## Status and interpretation

This is a **research handbook note**, not a completed experiment, clinical result, or evidence of diagnostic validity. Revisit the relevant method, cite primary research sources in a formal manuscript, and keep patient data outside Git.
