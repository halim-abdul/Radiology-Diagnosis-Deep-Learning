# Vision Transformers

## Scientific motivation

Patch tokenization enables global interactions but may require more data and positional handling.

## Mathematical or methodological point

Attention weights should not automatically be interpreted as medical explanations.

## Experimental design

Measure sample efficiency, domain shift and compute cost. When designing a benchmark, document the source population, dataset provenance, validation design, key assumptions, and whether this hypothesis has actually been tested.

## Status and interpretation

This is a **research handbook note**, not a completed experiment, clinical result, or evidence of diagnostic validity. Revisit the relevant method, cite primary research sources in a formal manuscript, and keep patient data outside Git.
