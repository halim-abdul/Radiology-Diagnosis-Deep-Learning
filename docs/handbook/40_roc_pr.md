# ROC and precision–recall evaluation

## Scientific motivation

AUROC summarizes ranking; average precision is sensitive to disease prevalence and positive retrieval.

## Concrete evaluation design

Report test prevalence, AUROC, average precision and confidence intervals. Compare with appropriate baselines. Describe the data-generating mechanism, clinical label semantics, statistical unit of analysis and evaluation conditions rather than treating every scan as independent.

## Research interpretation

Log model versions, random seeds and provenance. Analyze error cases, assumptions and possible distribution shift; perform external replication if evidence supports further investigation. **This is a research handbook entry—not completed experimental evidence or medical advice.**
