# Patient-level split design

## Scientific motivation

Repeated views, follow-up scans, and series from the same patient are statistical dependencies.

## Mathematical or methodological point

Partition by patient first; stratify only within grouping constraints.

## Experimental design

Time-wise and site-wise held-out testing are stronger evidence than random image splits. When designing a benchmark, document the source population, dataset provenance, validation design, key assumptions, and whether this hypothesis has actually been tested.

## Status and interpretation

This is a **research handbook note**, not a completed experiment, clinical result, or evidence of diagnostic validity. Revisit the relevant method, cite primary research sources in a formal manuscript, and keep patient data outside Git.
