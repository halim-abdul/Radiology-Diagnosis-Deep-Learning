# Label noise models

## Scientific motivation

Report-derived weak labels are different from specialist adjudication and may produce asymmetric false positives.

## Mathematical or methodological point

Under class-conditional noise, observed labels are corrupted through a confusion matrix.

## Experimental design

Measure reader disagreement and avoid treating inferred labels as ground truth. When designing a benchmark, document the source population, dataset provenance, validation design, key assumptions, and whether this hypothesis has actually been tested.

## Status and interpretation

This is a **research handbook note**, not a completed experiment, clinical result, or evidence of diagnostic validity. Revisit the relevant method, cite primary research sources in a formal manuscript, and keep patient data outside Git.
