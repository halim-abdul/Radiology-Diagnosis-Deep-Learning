# Research questions and hypotheses

## Scientific motivation

Define target label, imaging modality, cohort, deployment setting, target population, and clinically relevant failure costs before choosing a network.

## Mathematical or methodological point

A negative control tests for spurious hospital, scanner, or text marker correlations.

## Experimental design

Pre-register inclusion criteria and compare to a transparent baseline. When designing a benchmark, document the source population, dataset provenance, validation design, key assumptions, and whether this hypothesis has actually been tested.

## Status and interpretation

This is a **research handbook note**, not a completed experiment, clinical result, or evidence of diagnostic validity. Revisit the relevant method, cite primary research sources in a formal manuscript, and keep patient data outside Git.
