# Resampling and interpolation

## Scientific motivation

Nearest-neighbor interpolation usually preserves categorical mask labels; linear interpolation suits continuous image intensity.

## Mathematical or methodological point

Resampling changes apparent spacing and potentially anatomical borders.

## Experimental design

Quantify geometric interpolation error and retain original affines. When designing a benchmark, document the source population, dataset provenance, validation design, key assumptions, and whether this hypothesis has actually been tested.

## Status and interpretation

This is a **research handbook note**, not a completed experiment, clinical result, or evidence of diagnostic validity. Revisit the relevant method, cite primary research sources in a formal manuscript, and keep patient data outside Git.
