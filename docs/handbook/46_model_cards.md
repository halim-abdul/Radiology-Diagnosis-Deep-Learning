# Research model cards

## Scientific motivation

An interpretable model card records intended use, cohort scope, training resources, metrics and known limitations.

## Concrete evaluation design

Clearly label untrained weights and prohibit diagnosis claims. Compare with appropriate baselines. Describe the data-generating mechanism, clinical label semantics, statistical unit of analysis and evaluation conditions rather than treating every scan as independent.

## Research interpretation

Log model versions, random seeds and provenance. Analyze error cases, assumptions and possible distribution shift; perform external replication if evidence supports further investigation. **This is a research handbook entry—not completed experimental evidence or medical advice.**
