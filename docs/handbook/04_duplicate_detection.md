# Duplicate and near-duplicate leakage

## Scientific motivation

Perceptual and exact file hashes can reveal reused images, mirrored exports, and derived crops.

## Mathematical or methodological point

Hash consistency does not prove two distinct scans came from different patients.

## Experimental design

Check duplicate clusters before model development and retain auditable exclusion records. When designing a benchmark, document the source population, dataset provenance, validation design, key assumptions, and whether this hypothesis has actually been tested.

## Status and interpretation

This is a **research handbook note**, not a completed experiment, clinical result, or evidence of diagnostic validity. Revisit the relevant method, cite primary research sources in a formal manuscript, and keep patient data outside Git.
