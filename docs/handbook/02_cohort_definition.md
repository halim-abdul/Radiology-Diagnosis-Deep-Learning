# Cohort selection

## Scientific motivation

Inclusion and exclusion criteria change disease prevalence and external transportability.

## Mathematical or methodological point

Selection on scan availability can condition on a collider and introduce dataset bias.

## Experimental design

Publish a cohort diagram and counts for every exclusion. When designing a benchmark, document the source population, dataset provenance, validation design, key assumptions, and whether this hypothesis has actually been tested.

## Status and interpretation

This is a **research handbook note**, not a completed experiment, clinical result, or evidence of diagnostic validity. Revisit the relevant method, cite primary research sources in a formal manuscript, and keep patient data outside Git.
