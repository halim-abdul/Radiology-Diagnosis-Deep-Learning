# Multimodal fusion

## Scientific motivation

Metadata features may improve discrimination while introducing demographic, institutional or acquisition shortcuts.

## Mathematical or methodological point

Early, intermediate and late fusion make different assumptions about missingness.

## Experimental design

Run image-only, metadata-only and full-model ablations. When designing a benchmark, document the source population, dataset provenance, validation design, key assumptions, and whether this hypothesis has actually been tested.

## Status and interpretation

This is a **research handbook note**, not a completed experiment, clinical result, or evidence of diagnostic validity. Revisit the relevant method, cite primary research sources in a formal manuscript, and keep patient data outside Git.
