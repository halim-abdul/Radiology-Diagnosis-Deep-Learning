# Self-supervised representation learning

## Scientific motivation

Contrastive and masked-image objectives can leverage unlabeled scans before supervised adaptation.

## Mathematical or methodological point

Positive-pair construction must respect anatomical and patient identity.

## Experimental design

Compare against training-from-scratch under fixed data budgets. When designing a benchmark, document the source population, dataset provenance, validation design, key assumptions, and whether this hypothesis has actually been tested.

## Status and interpretation

This is a **research handbook note**, not a completed experiment, clinical result, or evidence of diagnostic validity. Revisit the relevant method, cite primary research sources in a formal manuscript, and keep patient data outside Git.
