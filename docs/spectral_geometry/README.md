# Spectral and Riemannian directions

An anatomical surface approximated as a Riemannian manifold has Laplace–Beltrami eigenpairs `-Δφ_k=λ_kφ_k`. Heat diffusion may be expressed spectrally as `H_t f=Σ_k exp(-tλ_k)〈f,φ_k〉φ_k`. From point clouds, graph Laplacians can approximate manifold operators under sampling and bandwidth assumptions. Research opportunities include multiscale heat signatures, scale-space lesion morphology, spectral stability, geometric cross-modal correspondence and physically meaningful 3D convolutions. **Not implemented; proposed mathematical research.**
